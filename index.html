Five habits that stop most attacks

  1. Use a password manager and a unique password for every account.
  2. Turn on multi-factor authentication, preferably with a passkey or security key.
  3. Install updates for your operating system, browser and apps promptly.
  4. Keep offline or versioned backups, and test restoring from them.
  5. Check links and attachments before opening, even from known senders.
